02 / Research system

From market context
to a testable thesis.

01

Read the wider market

Start with global cues, liquidity, volatility and the Indian market structure before looking at an individual company.

02

Track sector leadership

Compare relative strength, participation and momentum to understand where capital is moving—and where it is leaving.

03

Study the business

Balance growth, quality, valuation and risk. A chart without business context is incomplete; a story without numbers is noise.

04

Define the setup

Use price structure, volume and invalidation levels to turn an observation into a clear, testable research thesis.
